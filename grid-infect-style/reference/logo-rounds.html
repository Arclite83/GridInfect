Rules for A, ready for gen-assets.mjs
Letter grid4 units wide, 7 tall, 1 unit = 12 px at hero size. Bends are 90° or 45° only, one pad at every open trace end.
Etched wordTrace 3.5 px black 36% with a 1 px white 22% highlight offset −1.5 px. Pads r4.5 black 30% with r1.8 white 45% center. Contrast is raised over the substrate spec on purpose: substrate traces recede, a wordmark does not.
Lit wordSame geometry. infectLo 5.5 px under, infect 3 px over, white 35% 1 px core. Open ends are lit tips: glyphEdge r4.5 with r2.8 white. Glow 6 px at 60% and 18 px at 35%, both infect.
Sourcebug_E from the glyph set at 64 px, centered on the x-height, in the word gap. Its lit tip is the first point of the lit trace. One trace, no branching, enters the I.
SkinsOnly the lit word and the bug take the skin's infection hue. The etch is always tone on tone with the mask, so blue and breadboard skins get the logo for free.
MotionTitle screen: etch is present at rest, the bug lands, tip lights, then the lit word fills letter by letter left to right at one letter per hop. Same timing rule as board spread.
