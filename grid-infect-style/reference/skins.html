The three skins, same board

Same level, same placements, same bug queue. Only the skin layer moves: mask, copper and infection hue (STYLE-GUIDE §2). Values are the locked ones in out/tokens.json, mirrored by BoardPalette.Skins.Apply. The glass, the well, the blocker and the white tips are constant.

Reading the board. Dormant components carry a copper dot. Lit ones are the infection inside the glass with the 26 px near glow and 64 px halo. The half-lit cell mid-column shows a bleed entering from below. The shield tile is the blocker. The tray holds the next bug and two queued. Breadboard is the one skin whose mask is lighter than the glass, so its dormant components are a dark recess (space #4a3a22) rather than a white highlight.
